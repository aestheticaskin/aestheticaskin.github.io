About the Treatment

What is Laser Skin Resurfacing?

Laser skin resurfacing is a medical procedure that uses focused beams of light energy to remove damaged skin layers and stimulate the production of new collagen. The result is smoother, tighter, more evenly toned skin.

At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg selects the appropriate laser type — fractional, CO₂, Nd:YAG, or Q-switch — based on your specific skin concern, tone, and medical history. This ensures safe, effective outcomes for Indian skin tones which require specific settings to prevent post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation.

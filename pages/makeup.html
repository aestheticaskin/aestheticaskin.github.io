About the Procedure

What is Permanent Makeup?

Permanent makeup, also known as cosmetic tattooing or micropigmentation, is a medical aesthetic procedure that uses pigments implanted into the dermal layer to create the appearance of makeup.

At Aesthetica, all PMU procedures are performed under strict clinical supervision — ensuring your safety, hygiene, and natural-looking results every time.

Lasts 1–3 years Medically supervised Natural-looking results Touch-up friendly

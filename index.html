Frequently Asked Questions

We believe in transparency. Here are answers to the most common questions about our clinical approach.

Is there any downtime?

expand_more

Most of our treatments are designed to be minimally invasive. Depending on the procedure, downtime can range from zero to 48 hours. Your specialist will provide a detailed post-care guide.

Are treatments safe?

expand_more

Safety is our priority. All treatments are FDA-approved and performed under strict medical supervision using sterile equipment and clinical protocols.

How soon will I see results?

expand_more

Some treatments like hydration facials show immediate glow, while clinical skin cycles typically require 2-4 weeks to reveal cellular improvement.

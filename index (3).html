Frequently Asked Questions

We believe in full transparency. Below are answers to the most common questions about our clinical approach, safety standards, and what to expect at Aesthetica Skin Clinic, Pune.

Most treatments at Aesthetica are minimally invasive with zero to 48 hours of downtime. Laser therapies may cause mild redness for 24 hours; chemical peels may involve 2–5 days of gentle peeling. Your specialist provides a detailed post-care guide before every session.

Safety is our top priority. All procedures are FDA-approved and performed under strict medical supervision using sterile, single-use equipment and clinical-grade protocols. Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg oversees every treatment plan personally.

Hydration facials show an immediate glow. Clinical treatments for pigmentation, acne scars, or hair thinning typically require 2–4 weeks to reveal cellular improvement as the skin completes its natural regeneration cycle. Optimal results usually appear after a recommended series of sessions.

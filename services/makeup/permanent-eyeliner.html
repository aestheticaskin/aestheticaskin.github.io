About the Procedure

What is Permanent Eyeliner?

Permanent eyeliner is a cosmetic tattooing procedure using a digital machine to deposit pigment along the upper and/or lower lash line — creating defined, polished eyes that require no daily eyeliner application. Results last 2–4 years.

At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi discusses your desired liner style before treatment — from a subtle lash enhancement to a defined classic liner or smudged effect. The proximity of the eyelid to the eye makes medical supervision and sterility especially critical. All procedures use single-use, sterile cartridges in a clinical environment.

Available Styles

Lash Line Enhancement

Subtle pigment placed between the lashes — adds natural definition without a visible liner look. The most natural option.

Classic Liner

A defined line above the upper lash — thin to medium thickness. Clean, polished, and versatile for everyday wear.

Smudged / Smoky Effect

A softly blended liner that mimics a smudged liner effect — adds depth and drama while still looking effortless.

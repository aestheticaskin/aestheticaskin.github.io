About the Procedure

What is Lip Blush?

Lip blush is a semi-permanent cosmetic tattooing technique that uses a digital machine to deposit a soft, natural wash of pigment across the lips — enhancing their natural colour, defining the lip border, and creating the appearance of fuller, more symmetrical lips without any harsh or artificial look.

Unlike traditional lip liner tattoos (which often appear dark and block-coloured), lip blush creates a subtle, watercolour-like tint that looks like your most beautiful natural lip colour — permanently. At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi selects pigment shades that complement your skin undertone and natural lip colour, and the lip border is designed to enhance symmetry before any pigment is applied.

Important: History of Cold Sores

If you have a history of cold sores (herpes simplex virus), any lip procedure can trigger an outbreak. To prevent this, Dr. Prachi will prescribe an antiviral medication (such as aciclovir) to be taken starting 2 days before your appointment and continued for 3–5 days after. Please disclose your history at the consultation — this is essential for safe treatment.

Common Questions

Lip Blush — FAQ

Everything you need to know before your lip blush appointment at Aesthetica, Punawale, Pune.

Lip blush deposits a soft watercolour wash of natural pigment across the lips — enhancing colour, defining the border, and creating fuller-looking, symmetrical lips. Unlike old lip liner tattoos, the result looks like your best natural lip colour, not drawn-on makeup.

Lip blush lasts 1–3 years depending on skin type, diet, sun exposure, and aftercare. Lips exfoliate faster than other areas, so colour refreshes every 1–2 years keep results looking vibrant. The 4–6 week touch-up included in your treatment is essential for the final result.

Medical numbing cream is applied for 30–45 minutes before. Lips are more sensitive than brows, so some mild discomfort or tingling is possible, but most clients find it very manageable. Additional numbing can be applied mid-session if needed.

Lips appear 30–50% darker immediately after — this is completely normal. Over 5–10 days the colour lightens significantly to reveal the true blush result. Mild flaking is expected. Do not pick the skin. Final results are fully visible at 4 weeks, after the included touch-up session.

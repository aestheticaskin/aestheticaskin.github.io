About the Procedure

What are Ombré Powder Brows?

Ombré powder brows use a digital permanent makeup machine to deposit pigment in a soft stippling pattern across the brow — creating a gradient effect that is lighter at the front and gradually deeper and more defined toward the tail. The result mimics the look of brow powder or eyeshadow applied by a professional makeup artist.

Unlike microblading's manual hair-stroke technique, powder brows use a machine — making them more suitable for all skin types including oily skin, and delivering longer-lasting results of 2–3 years. At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi maps the ideal brow shape for your face before beginning, ensuring perfect symmetry and a colour that complements your natural tone.

Ombré Powder Brows — FAQ

Everything you need to know before your appointment at Aesthetica, Punawale, Pune.

Ombré powder brows use a digital machine to create a soft gradient brow — lighter at the front, gradually deeper toward the tail — mimicking the look of professionally filled-in brows with powder. Results last 2–3 years.

Choose powder brows for: oily skin, a filled/defined makeup look, longer-lasting results (2–3 yr), or if you already have existing permanent makeup. Choose microblading for: a very natural hair-like result, dry to normal skin, and if you want a more subtle, no-makeup look. Dr. Prachi will guide you at the consultation.

Yes — powder brows are actually the recommended technique for oily skin types. The machine stippling technique retains shape and colour far better than microblading hair strokes, which can blur and heal patchy on oilier skin types.

Brows appear significantly darker immediately after — up to 40–50% darker than the final healed result. This is completely normal. Over the 10–14 day healing period, the brows will lighten, soften, and reveal their true colour. This initial darkness surprises many clients who are not expecting it, so do plan for this.

In many cases, yes. Ombré powder brows can be applied over faded microblading to refresh and enhance the look. Dr. Prachi will assess your existing pigment at the consultation and advise whether correction work is needed first.

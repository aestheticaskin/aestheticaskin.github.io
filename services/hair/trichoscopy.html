About the Assessment

What is Trichoscopy?

Trichoscopy is a non-invasive diagnostic technique that uses a handheld dermatoscope with polarised light to magnify the scalp and individual hair follicles at 10x–50x resolution. It allows Dr. Prachi to see structures invisible to the naked eye — follicle density, miniaturisation patterns, scalp vascularity, hair shaft irregularities, and surface signs of scalp disease.

At Aesthetica, trichoscopy is recommended as the essential first step for every patient presenting with hair loss, hair thinning, or scalp concerns. Different types of alopecia require entirely different treatments — treating without a precise diagnosis wastes time, money, and can worsen certain conditions. Trichoscopy removes the guesswork entirely.

Trichoscopy — FAQ

Everything you need to know about hair and scalp diagnosis at Aesthetica, Punawale, Pune.

Trichoscopy is a non-invasive clinical diagnostic technique using a dermatoscope to magnify the scalp and hair follicles at high resolution. It allows Dr. Prachi to precisely identify the type and cause of hair loss before designing a treatment plan — eliminating guesswork and ensuring you receive the right treatment from day one.

Trichoscopy is completely painless and non-invasive. A small handheld device is gently pressed against the scalp surface — no needles, no incisions, no discomfort. The entire assessment takes 20–30 minutes and results are discussed the same day.

Trichoscopy can diagnose androgenetic alopecia, alopecia areata, telogen effluvium, scarring alopecias (LPP, FFA, CCCA), trichotillomania, seborrheic dermatitis, scalp psoriasis, folliculitis, and hair shaft disorders — each with distinct trichoscopic signatures.

Blood tests identify systemic causes of hair loss (thyroid dysfunction, iron deficiency, hormonal imbalances), while trichoscopy directly assesses the scalp and follicles. Both are complementary — Dr. Prachi will advise whether blood investigations are also needed based on your trichoscopy findings and clinical history.

Yes — at Aesthetica, trichoscopy is performed before commencing any hair restoration treatment. It confirms that PRP or scalp microneedling is appropriate for your specific type of hair loss, and identifies the exact zones to target for best results. It also establishes a baseline to measure progress.

About the Treatment

What is Meso Hair Therapy?

Meso Hair Therapy (also called Hair Mesotherapy) delivers a precisely formulated cocktail of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, hyaluronic acid, and hair-growth peptides directly into the scalp dermis via multiple fine micro-injections. By bypassing the skin barrier entirely, these potent ingredients reach the hair follicle at therapeutic concentrations — something oral supplements and topical serums cannot achieve.

At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg customises the meso cocktail for each patient based on their trichoscopy findings, scalp condition, and hair loss type. The formulation may include biotin, B-complex vitamins, zinc, copper, silicon, DHT-blocking peptides, and hyaluronic acid — adjusted at each session based on your progress and scalp response.

Common Questions

Meso Hair Therapy — FAQ

Answers to the most common questions about hair mesotherapy at Aesthetica, Punawale, Pune.

Meso Hair Therapy delivers a customised cocktail of vitamins, minerals, amino acids, and DHT-blocking peptides directly into the scalp via fine micro-injections. By bypassing the skin barrier, these ingredients reach hair follicles at concentrations far higher than oral supplements or topical serums can achieve.

The cocktail is customised by Dr. Prachi based on your scalp diagnosis and typically includes biotin, B-complex vitamins, zinc, copper, silicon, amino acids, hyaluronic acid, and DHT-blocking peptides. The formulation is adjusted at each session based on your scalp's response and progress.

PRP uses your own blood's growth factors to biologically stimulate follicles. Meso delivers nutritional and pharmaceutical ingredients directly to the scalp. They work via different mechanisms and are frequently combined for superior results — Dr. Prachi will advise which approach suits your specific hair loss type.

Topical numbing cream is applied 30 minutes before the procedure. Most patients feel a mild pricking sensation from the micro-injections — not significantly painful. The scalp may be slightly tender for 12–24 hours after treatment, which resolves on its own.

A standard protocol of 6–8 sessions spaced 2 weeks apart is recommended. Reduced hair fall is typically noticeable from sessions 3–4. New hair growth becomes visible from session 5–6. Maintenance sessions every 4–6 weeks sustain long-term results.

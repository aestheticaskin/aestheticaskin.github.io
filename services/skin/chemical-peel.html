About the Treatment

What is a Chemical Peel?

A chemical peel applies a carefully selected acid solution to the skin surface, dissolving the bonds that hold dead and damaged skin cells together. As these layers shed, fresh skin with improved tone and texture is revealed beneath.

At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi Gupta Garg chooses from superficial, medium-depth, and deep peel formulations based on your exact skin concern, Fitzpatrick skin type, and treatment goals — ensuring both safety and effectiveness for Indian skin tones.

Common Questions

Chemical Peel — FAQ

Everything you need to know before your chemical peel at Aesthetica Skin Clinic, Punawale, Pune.

A chemical peel applies a medical-grade acid solution to exfoliate damaged outer skin layers, stimulating cell renewal to reveal clearer, smoother, more evenly toned skin beneath.

Dr. Prachi selects the peel based on your concern: salicylic for acne, glycolic/lactic for dullness and fine lines, TCA for deeper pigmentation and scarring. A consultation is required before any peel treatment.

Superficial peels have 24–48 hours of mild redness. Medium-depth peels result in visible shedding and peeling for 5–7 days. You will receive a complete aftercare guide and should avoid sun exposure and active skincare during recovery.

Yes. Dr. Prachi specialises in treating Indian and South Asian skin. She carefully selects peel strengths and formulations that are safe for Fitzpatrick III–V skin types, minimising risk of hyperpigmentation or scarring.

A series of 4–6 sessions spaced 2–4 weeks apart is typically recommended. Maintenance peels can be done monthly. The exact number depends on your skin concern and the depth of peel used.

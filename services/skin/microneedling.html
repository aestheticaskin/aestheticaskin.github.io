About the Treatment

What is Microneedling?

Microneedling — also known as Collagen Induction Therapy (CIT) — uses a medical-grade device with ultra-fine sterile needles to create thousands of controlled micro-channels in the skin. This stimulates the skin's natural wound-healing process, triggering production of new collagen and elastin.

At Aesthetica, Dr. Prachi uses advanced automated microneedling devices (Dermapen) with adjustable needle depth — allowing precision targeting of specific concerns from superficial texture to deeper acne scars. PRP can be combined for significantly enhanced results.

Common Questions

Microneedling — FAQ

Answers to the most commonly asked questions about microneedling at Aesthetica, Punawale, Pune.

Microneedling uses a device with fine sterile needles to create micro-injuries in the skin, triggering the natural healing response to produce new collagen and elastin. This improves scars, texture, pores, fine lines, and overall skin quality over a series of sessions.

Topical numbing cream is applied 30–45 minutes before treatment. Most patients describe it as a mild prickling or vibrating sensation — not painful. The face may feel warm during and immediately after the session.

Medical Dermapen devices used at Aesthetica create vertical micro-channels at precise controlled depths. Home dermarollers roll at an angle, causing more tearing and are not clinically equivalent. Professional microneedling is significantly safer and more effective.

Yes — combining microneedling with PRP (Platelet-Rich Plasma) supercharges results. Growth factors in PRP penetrate the micro-channels created during needling, significantly boosting collagen production and accelerating skin repair. Ask Dr. Prachi about this combination.

Initial improvement in skin texture and radiance is visible within 2–4 weeks of your first session. Significant collagen remodelling — particularly for scars — typically becomes visible after 3–4 sessions and continues improving for up to 6 months post-treatment.

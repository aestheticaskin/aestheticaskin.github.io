About the Treatment

What is Skin Brightening Treatment?

Skin brightening treatment at Aesthetica is a clinically designed multi-modality approach to correcting hyperpigmentation — encompassing melasma, post-acne marks, sun spots, and general uneven tone — using a combination of laser technology, medical-grade peels, and prescription topical agents.

Unlike cosmetic lightening creams available over-the-counter, clinical brightening treatments penetrate deeper into the dermis to address the root cause of pigmentation — targeting overactive melanocytes with medical precision. Dr. Prachi designs a protocol unique to your pigmentation type, depth, and skin tone, especially important for Indian and South Asian skin types which are more pigmentation-prone.

An Important Note on Skin Brightening

Skin brightening at Aesthetica is about correcting uneven pigmentation and restoring your natural, healthy skin tone — not about changing your fundamental skin colour. We do not offer bleaching procedures. Every treatment is designed to enhance your natural beauty safely and ethically.

Skin Brightening — FAQ

Commonly asked questions about pigmentation and skin brightening treatment at Aesthetica, Punawale, Pune.

Pigmentation results from excess melanin production triggered by sun exposure, hormonal changes, acne inflammation, aging, or injury. In India, UV exposure is the most common cause, and darker skin tones are inherently more prone to pigmentation changes.

Melasma is best treated with a combination of Q-switch laser sessions, glycolic or TCA peels, and prescription topicals (hydroquinone, kojic acid, azelaic acid). Strict daily SPF 50+ is non-negotiable. Dr. Prachi specialises in melasma management for South Asian skin at Aesthetica, Punawale.
